Frequently asked questions

Does the app read payment cards?
No. EMV Lexicon is an educational reference and parsing tool. It does not read cards or handle real cardholder data.

Can I use real keys or card data?
No. Use test vectors and test keys only.

Does the app collect my data?
No account is needed. Bookmarks and theme settings are stored on your device.
