EMV Lexicon – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy applies to the EMV Lexicon Android app (package com.voltexdevelopments.emv), published by Voltex Developments ("we", "us").

Summary

EMV Lexicon does not require an account, and it does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal data. The app requests no special device permissions (such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage). It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your preferences and in-app state (for example, settings or bookmarks) locally on your device using Android's standard app storage. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Any text you enter into the app's tools (for example, the DE55 parser) is processed on your device only. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party.

Third-party services

The app may download font files from Google Fonts. Such a request is made directly to Google and may expose standard technical information, such as your IP address, to Google. See Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive this information.

Data sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties.

Children

The app is a technical reference for payment professionals and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's own protections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
